# DCSt SACNAS 2026 References

Sources for the talk "A Quantum Circuit Approach to Continuous Aircraft Stall Risk Assessment" by Samuel A. Márquez González, Rutgers University–Camden, SACNAS NDiSTEM 2026.

## Statistics and accident reports

1. L. Westfall, "Causes and contributing factors in general aviation fixed-wing stall and spin accidents," undergraduate honors thesis, University of Arkansas, Fayetteville, 2025.
2. AOPA Air Safety Institute, "Stall and spin accidents: Keep the wings flying," special report, Frederick, MD, 2017.
3. Bureau d'Enquêtes et d'Analyses pour la sécurité de l'aviation civile, final report on the accident on 1 June 2009 to the Airbus A330-203 registered F-GZCP, Air France flight AF 447 Rio de Janeiro–Paris, July 2012. [FAA copy of the report](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/f-cp090601.en_.pdf)
4. Bureau d'Enquêtes et d'Analyses, "Stall after takeoff in icing conditions, collision with the ground, fire," final report VP-CAZ, Beechcraft Premier 1A near Annemasse Aerodrome, 4 March 2013, Le Bourget, 2014. Case A.
5. Komite Nasional Keselamatan Transportasi, "Aircraft accident investigation report: PT Sriwijaya Air, Boeing 737-500, PK-CLC," KNKT.21.01.01.04, Jakarta, 2022. Case B.
6. Junta Investigadora de Accidentes de Aviación Civil, "Informe final: Accidente del avión ATR 42-320, matrícula YV1010, operado por CONVIASA, 13 de septiembre de 2010," expediente 036/2010, Venezuela, 2014. Case C.

References 1 and 2 support the figure that stall causes nearly 15% of fatal fixed-wing accidents.

## Standards, tools and methods

7. U.S. Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14, § 25.207, "Stall warning." Basis for the classical detector.
8. Laminar Research, X-Plane 11 flight simulator. Used to reconstruct all five scenarios.
9. A. Javadi-Abhari et al., "Quantum computing with Qiskit," arXiv:2405.08810, 2024. Circuits built with Qiskit 2.3.1 and simulated with Qiskit Aer 0.17.2.

## Quote and manuscript

10. R. P. Feynman, "Simulating physics with computers," International Journal of Theoretical Physics 21, 467–488, 1982. Quoted on the Conclusion slide.
11. S. A. Márquez González, "DCSt: A quantum kernel for aircraft stall risk assessment," manuscript in preparation, 2026. Source of every DCSt result in the talk.
